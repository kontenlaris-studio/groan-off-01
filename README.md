One original dad joke for [The One-Liner Groan-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-the-one-liner-groan-off-5a1febd5) on make.fun — a single line of at most 140 characters, the kind of pun a dad delivers at the dinner table.

> The alarm clock retired after 6 years — it said it was tired of being snoozed on.

Six years of 5am starts, and the punch lands on the word the whole joke was built for: "snoozed on" — the snooze button, and being ignored, in the same breath.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
